# Asking the owner — patterns

The owner's time is the scarcest thing in the MVP. Ask everything that matters, nothing
you could find out yourself, and make each answer one click when you can.

## Order

1. Scope: whole areas the design draws but nothing serves (lists, payments, a social
   feed). Build now, later, or cut.
2. Truth: promises the design makes that the product cannot keep (encryption, data
   location, "we never store…"). Reword, build, or remove.
3. Rules: who may do what, what happens on error, limits, notifications.
4. Details per screen: undrawn behaviour, contradictions between boards.

## One question

- Names the screen and the item ids it decides ("Calendar · LP-CAL-12..15").
- Says what is unclear in one sentence, then the options. Recommendation first, marked
  "(Recommended)", with its reason and size: S (hours), M (a day), L (days, new domain or
  outside service).
- Options are real alternatives; "Other" is always there for the owner's own words.
- A picture helps: put the board's text or a short mock in the option's preview.

## Rounds

- Up to 4 questions per card; send the next card right after the answers. Ten cards in a
  row is fine when the design is large.
- After each card write the answers into `decisions.md` before the next one.
- An answer that opens new questions adds them to the next card.
- Mid-build, ask the moment the plan or the design stops deciding — do not build around
  the gap and ask later.
